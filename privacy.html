Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 31, 2024

Zynaty Tech ("we", "our", or "us") built OminiControl as a smart TV remote control application. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our app.

Summary: OminiControl operates entirely on your local network. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers.

Information We Do NOT Collect

OminiControl is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • Usage analytics or tracking data
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers for advertising
  • Any data transmitted to external servers

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The following information is stored only on your device and never leaves it:

  • TV Device Information: Names, IP addresses, and connection details of TVs you connect to
  • Pairing Credentials: Authentication keys required to control your TV, stored securely in iOS Keychain
  • App Preferences: Your settings and preferences within the app

Local Network Access

OminiControl requires access to your local network to:

  • Discover smart TVs on your WiFi network
  • Establish connections with your TV for remote control
  • Send commands to control your TV

All communication happens directly between your iPhone/iPad and your TV over your local WiFi network. No data passes through our servers.

Third-Party Services

OminiControl does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Data Security

We take security seriously:

  • Pairing credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain, Apple's secure storage system
  • All TV communication uses standard protocols (WebSocket, mDNS, SSDP)
  • No data is transmitted over the internet

Children's Privacy

OminiControl does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The app is safe for use by all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
